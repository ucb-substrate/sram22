## What each trace is

| Trace | Node | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CLK** | clock | The access begins on its rising edge. |
| **PC_B** | precharge bar | Low precharges/equalizes the bitlines; goes high to start the access. |
| **WLEN** | wordline enable | The self-timed enable that gates the decoded wordline. |
| **WL[0]** | wordline | The selected row's wordline; its pulse connects the bitcells to the bitlines. |
| **BL[0] / BR[0]** | bitline pair | The true/complement bitlines of column 0; they separate as the cell discharges one side. |
| **SAEN** | sense-amp enable | Fires the sense amplifier once enough bitline differential has built up. |

## Reading the sequence

1. On the **rising clock edge**, `PC_B` rises, ending precharge and releasing
   the bitlines from VDD.
2. `WLEN` asserts and the decoded **wordline** `WL[0]` pulses high, connecting
   the addressed row's cells to their bitlines.
3. The accessed cell pulls one of **BL[0] / BR[0]** down while the other stays
   near VDD — the differential you can see opening between the red and olive
   traces.
4. `SAEN` fires the sense amplifier, which resolves the small differential to a
   full-swing logic level and latches the result toward `dout`.
5. The wordline turns off and `PC_B` falls again to precharge for the next
   cycle.

## Replica vs. main bitline

The crucial timing question is *when to turn the wordline off and fire the sense
amp*. SRAM22 answers it with a **replica bitline** rather than a fixed delay.

- The **main bitlines** (`BL`/`BR` above) carry the actual read data. Their
  differential must be large enough for the sense amp — but no larger, or time
  and energy are wasted.
- A **replica bitline**, loaded by a small group of always-on replica cells,
  discharges at a rate that tracks the real bitlines across process, voltage,
  and temperature. When the replica bitline falls to ≈ VDD/2, the control logic
  turns `WLEN` off and asserts `SAEN`.

Because the replica tracks the real array, the wordline pulse self-adjusts: it
is just long enough to develop a reliable sense margin in every corner, instead
of being padded for the worst case.
